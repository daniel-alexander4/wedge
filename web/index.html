Welcome to wedge

Complete these steps to get started.

1 Configure SSH Key

wedge signs with the SSH keys already in your ~/.ssh directory. Keys loaded into your ssh-agent are used automatically — any of them can sign into any site.

2 Add Target Systems

Add the first site (URL) wedge may sign into. wedge will offer to add any other site the first time you sign into it.

3 Ready

You're all set. wedge is ready to sign login requests from your configured target systems.
